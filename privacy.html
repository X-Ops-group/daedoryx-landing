PREVIEW / PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy & data handling

This prototype is a static marketing and event-presentation website. It does not provide an account system or configured server-side lead intake.

Contact form

Details entered into the enquiry generator remain in your browser. When you click “Copy enquiry brief,” the page attempts to copy the text to your device clipboard. It does not submit the form to our servers. You choose whether, where, and to whom to send the copied text.

Local preferences and third parties

The language toggle stores a preference in localStorage under daedoryx-language. This prototype does not install marketing analytics or advertising trackers. The site currently requests fonts from Google's font servers; those requests may disclose network and browser metadata to the font provider. Static hosting infrastructure may separately keep access logs.

Publication status

This information describes the current preview implementation, not a finalized legal privacy notice. The responsible legal entity, contact information, applicable notices, and any future lead processing must be confirmed before public commercial launch.
